How it works

1

Import transactions

Upload your CSV and select a provider preset (WEX, Comdata, or manual mapping). The tool validates:

  • Date format and range
  • Fuel type presence
  • Volume and unit fields
  • Vehicle ID structure
2

Map & validate

The tool flags issues inline:

⚠ "Unrecognized fuel_type at Row 42; map it."
⚠ "Volume unit missing; choose gal or L."
⚠ "Negative or zero volume at Row 108; review entry."

Fix them before computing emissions.

3

Compute emissions

For each transaction: co2e_kg = volume × factor(fuel_type, kg/gal or kg/L)

Factor versioning: EPA Emission Factors Hub 2025, AR5-100 GWP basis.

Intensity (optional): If odometer data is present, compute kg CO₂e per mile.

4

Export and share

Download PDF and CSV reports. Every export includes:

  • Factor source and date (EPA Emission Factors Hub, Jan 2025)
  • Version lock for auditability
  • GWP basis (AR5-100)
  • Methodology link (GHG Protocol Corporate Standard)
